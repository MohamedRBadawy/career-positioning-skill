# Market Search Templates

Use these to extend the core Step 3 searches when a session needs deeper
market intelligence. Replace bracketed terms with the session's target role,
field, and the current year. Always run the core Step 3 set first. These are
expansions, not replacements.

## 1. Role demand: deeper cuts

- "[target role] job description" site:linkedin.com/jobs. Read 5 to 10 real
  postings, not summaries.
- "[target role] vs [adjacent role]" difference [year]. Does the market split
  the role?
- "companies hiring [target role] remote" [year]
- "[target role] interview questions" [year]: what employers actually test
- "skills gap [target field]" report [year]: what employers say they cannot find

## 2. Compensation and rates

- "[target role] freelance hourly rate" [currency] [year]
- "[target role] retainer pricing" small business
- "fractional [target role] rates" [year]: relevant if the independent track
  is live
- "[target role] salary" remote [region A] vs [region B] [year]: a
  location-arbitrage check between where the user lives and where clients or
  employers are

## 3. Tools and technology

- "[tool the user relies on] vs [rising competitor]" [year]: is the current
  stack still the market's choice?
- "most requested automation tools" [target field] [year]
- "AI agent tools [target field] teams" [year]
- "[target field] tech stack" job postings [year]

## 4. Automation risk

- "AI replacing [target role] tasks" [year]
- "what [target role]s still do that AI can't" [year]
- "[target field] AI adoption" survey [year]: how fast clients adopt AI
  themselves, which reframes what they will pay a person for

## 5. Competitive and positioning landscape

- "what makes a strong [target role] portfolio" [year]
- "[target role] case study" examples: the proof format the market responds to
- "[service type] consultant positioning" [client segment]
- "build in public [target field]": which visibility formats work now

## 6. Regional and remote

- "[target role] remote" [time zone] friendly [year]
- "hiring remote contractors [user's country]" payment methods: cross-border
  payment friction is a common hidden constraint
- "[target role] [target region] market" demand [year]: test whether a
  nearby region is a realistic client or employer base

## Synthesis discipline

Three lists, then stop searching:

1. What the market values most in this role now
2. What is rising: learn now, advantage in 6 to 12 months
3. What is declining: deprioritize or reframe

If a result contradicts the user's profile or a prior Grooya session, surface
the contradiction explicitly. Do not silently average it away.
